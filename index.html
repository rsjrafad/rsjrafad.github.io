>about

From the culture plate to the command line.

I am a microbiology graduate of Jashore University of Science and Technology (JUST), Bangladesh, and work in the Microbial Biodiversity and Bioinformatics Laboratory. My research sits where wet-lab microbiology meets computational biology.

My undergraduate thesis characterised cancer-associated mutations in the anti-apoptotic protein MCL-1 and how they change its binding to BAK. I have also contributed to the first genomic report of epidemic clade 2.5.6 Acinetobacter baumannii from hospital surfaces in Bangladesh, and I build Linux pipelines for genome assembly, pangenome and resistome analysis.

Before moving into bioinformatics, I worked as a project assistant in the General Microbiology Lab at JUST on microbial contamination of sports clothing and the antibacterial effect of chitosan-treated fabric.

What I work on

Bacterial genomics and Metagenomics

Assembling and annotating bacterial genomes to trace lineages, resistance genes and pangenome structure, plus 16S rRNA amplicon analysis from raw reads to taxonomic profiles.
